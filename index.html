About Nicolettuce

Nicolettuce is a small farm-to-table business that grows and sells a variety of fresh, hydroponic lettuce. Unlike most lettuce you’ll find in the market, ours is 100% free from insecticides and pesticides — clean, crisp, and safe to eat straight from the pack.

What makes Nicolettuce even more unique is how it started. It was built by a 16-year-old girl during the pandemic, driven by her curiosity about how plants can grow without soil. While staying at home, she began researching hydroponics — a modern, soil-free way of growing plants using water and nutrients. She discovered that this method was common in Western countries but not yet popular in the Philippines.

Because of her curiosity and determination, she decided to try it herself. The beginning wasn’t easy — there were challenges, mistakes, and moments of doubt. But she never gave up. Through patience, learning, and love for growing something healthy, Nicolettuce was born — a passion project that turned into a local source of fresh, clean, and sustainable greens.

At Nicolettuce, every leaf is grown with care — no chemicals, no shortcuts, just pure freshness from our small hydroponic garden to your table.
